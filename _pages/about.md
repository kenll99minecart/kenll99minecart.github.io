I'm a first year PhD student in [Helmholtz Munich](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/), supervised by [Dr. Malte Luecken](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/icb/malte-luecken) and [Prof. Fabian Theis](https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/icb/fabian-theis). My current research project involving construction of a sc(single-cell) kidney organoid atlas for organoid fidelity measures and large-scale perturbation experiment. I am enthusiatic in different deep learning methodlogies to aid biologists for the discovery of novel biomarkers and targets for therapeutic processes, with a particular interest in perturbation and virtual cell modeling.

Previously I was a Mphil student majoring in bioengineering at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://cbe.hkust.edu.hk/), supervised by [**Prof. Henry Lam**](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/henry-hei-ning-lam). We created [SpectroVQ](https://github.com/kenll99minecart/SpectroVQ/tree/main), the first deep learning method to lossy compress mass spectrometry-based proteomics data. I also worked on electrical impedance tomography under the supervision of [Dr. Russell Chan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=list_works&hl=en&hl=en&user=ABEByy0AAAAJ) in [E-SENSE Innovation & Technology](https://www.esense-inno.tech/)

During my undergraduate studies, I embarked in the journey of numerous research projects, including the [iGEM competition](https://2021.igem.org/Team:HKUST), research internship projects on developing machine learning pipeline/model for immune cell activity and medical image analysis with portable Electrical Impedance Tomography.
